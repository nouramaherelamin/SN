## Important limitation
The uploaded image is a single flattened PNG, not a layered design source. Therefore the child and rover transparent PNGs are automatic approximate extractions and may contain small background fragments or missing edge details. The parchment, navigation, button, feature bar, and magnifying glass are rectangular crops and still include any pixels that overlap them in the screenshot.

For a production-ready website, use these as visual references/assets and rebuild text, navigation, buttons, cards, and interactive controls in HTML/CSS. Keep the supplied Oppy/child artwork, proportions, facial features, and original warm Mars palette unchanged. Do not use the whole screenshot as one unresponsive background.
